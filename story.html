Our Story

THE JOURNEY BEGINS

In 1971, Vihreä was founded in Helsinki, Finland by Jooseppi and Anja Korhonen. As a vegan couple, they made it their mission to provide more inclusive dining options for vegetarians and vegans alike. The initial menu was very limited in its vegetarian options, only offering a few dishes inspired by Finnish culture.

GROWTH

Utilizing locally grown produce and seasonal ingredients provided fantastic flavor and the food quickly gained popularity from both locals and visitors. As the business grew, so did the menu, offering a taste for every palate. We started a carry-out service to meet demand for more convenient meals and a catering service for large venues.

COMMUNITY SUPPORT

Vihreä was a pioneer not only in menu options, but also in sustainability. From supporting local farmers, to innovative zero waste practices, we never forgot our commitment to sustainability.

THE VIHREÄ APPROACH

Farm to Table

From the beginning, our founders were determined to use quality ingredients from reliable sources. The vast majority of our produce is sourced from local farms, which helps support local economies and reduce the environmental impact of long-distance food transportation. Our farm-to-table approach includes organic materials, and we avoid preservatives and additives as much as possible. Utilizing local farms also allows us to ensure that our ingredients are sourced responsibly, with fair labor practices and minimal environmental harm.

Sustainability and Efficiency

Expanding our business to new locations and countries has made us more aware of how our facilities affect the environment. Because of this, we have begun to implement practices to limit greenhouse gas emissions, unnecessary energy consumption, and food waste. Our new buildings are equipped with energy-efficient lighting and appliances, and we are working to implement these tools in our existing locations. While not everything can be served to our guests, we work daily to ensure that food waste is reused. Each location composts extra produce, which is then donated to the local farms that source our ingredients.

Preparation Processes

Within our kitchens, we strive to produce healthy, balanced meals that can satisfy any guest’s taste. Each individual location offers seasonal menus to get the most out of regional crops at peak freshness, creating the best flavor and nutritional value possible in our dishes. Our chefs experiment daily, creating new, delicious dishes that we are proud to share with our patrons.
